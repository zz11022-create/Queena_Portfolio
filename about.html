About

Hi, I’m Queena.

I’m Zhaoquan Zheng, an AI product designer and creative technologist based in New York. I’m currently studying at NYU ITP. My background in product design and Design Informatics at the University of Edinburgh taught me to move between what people say, what the data shows, and what I can put in someone’s hands.

On a client project for Moonsift, I used Python to analyze how people saved and organized products across more than 20,000 user records. At NYU, my team worked with The Center for Discovery to prototype a simple cue that helps staff notice and transfer custom orthotics when children change shoes. Both projects started with the same question: what would make someone’s next step easier?

That question also led to Career Mixtape, where international students can turn a career story into something to try, and Pet Court, a playful experiment in how AI can surface multiple interpretations of the same event. I like building these ideas far enough that someone can try them and tell me what I missed.

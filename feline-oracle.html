Feline Oracle

A cat tarot experiment that uses gestures to shuffle and draw cards.

Try the interactive demo ↗

Make a physical gesture part of a digital ritual

Participants can wave to shuffle or hover over a card to draw it. The portfolio demo also supports manual controls and shows preset readings.

Give each input a purpose

Voice or text sets the question, hand motion performs the shuffle, and a hidden card introduces chance. Camera calibration becomes part of the ritual through a virtual paw.

Let people complete the ritual their way

The original explored a Gemini-generated interpretation. This browser edition uses preset readings and supports mouse and text throughout.

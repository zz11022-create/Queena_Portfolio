INFORITANCE

A speculative installation that gives the transmission of information a physical form through touch, light, and sculptural modules.

Watch the project film ↗

An interaction in stages

Touch starts a sequence of lights across the installation. Each illuminated module makes the passage of the signal visible.

Making the object

An Arduino Uno controls the light response. The process boards show the interaction trigger, fabrication, and assembly.

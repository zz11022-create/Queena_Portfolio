From ingredients to a small action

Add ingredients and review what is closest to expiring. The prototype uses those items to assemble a fortune and an intentionally absurd recipe from preset combinations.

Design the return visit

The fortune gives people a reason to open the inventory again. The ingredient list keeps the practical task visible beside the playful output.

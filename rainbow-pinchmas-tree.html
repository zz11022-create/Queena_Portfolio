Pinch to make

Hand tracking maps the thumb and index finger to a particle emitter. As the grains pile up, the system looks for a stable triangular shape.

Reveal the tree

When the pile meets the shape rule, its particles lift into a holographic tree. Gifts underneath switch between short pieces of music.
